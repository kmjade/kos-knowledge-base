# LLM Research

> **Area:** Ongoing tracking of large language model developments, principles, and applications.

## Overview

LLMs (Large Language Models) are the core technology direction in AI today. This area tracks LLM architecture evolution, training methods, engineering practices, and knowledge integration trends.
