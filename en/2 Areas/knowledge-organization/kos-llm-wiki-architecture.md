# KOS+LLM-Wiki Architecture v1.0

> Knowledge Organization Systems x LLM-Wiki Compilation Engine
> UDC 025.4

This is the English mirror of [[KOS+LLM-Wiki 架构设计说明书|KOS+LLM-Wiki 架构设计说明书 (CN)]]. See the CN version for full content.

## Overview

The KOS+LLM-Wiki architecture defines a five-layer knowledge organization system:

1. **Metadata Layer** (Frontmatter + Dublin Core)
2. **Classification Layer** (DDC, UDC, CLC, LCC)
3. **Thesaurus Layer** (SKOS)
4. **Ontology Layer** (OWL/RDF)
5. **Knowledge Graph Layer** (Concepts, Entities, Sources)
