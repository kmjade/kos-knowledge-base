# Documentation

> UDC 002 — Documentation, documents, books.

## Definition

Documentation science is the field of study concerned with the recording, organization, retrieval, and dissemination of information. In the UDC system, 002 covers the methodological foundations of documentation. In modern knowledge management, documentation encompasses all forms of recorded information — from paper documents to digital knowledge bases.
