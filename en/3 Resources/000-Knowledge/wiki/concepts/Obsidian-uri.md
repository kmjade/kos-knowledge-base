# Obsidian URI

> `obsidian://` protocol — URL scheme for cross-app linking and automation.

## Definition

Obsidian URI is the URL scheme (`obsidian://`) for the Obsidian note-taking app, allowing direct opening of specific notes, executing commands, or searching content from browsers, apps, or scripts.

## Common Usage

```
obsidian://open?vault=MyVault&file=NotePath
obsidian://search?vault=MyVault&query=search terms
obsidian://new?vault=MyVault&name=NewNote
```
