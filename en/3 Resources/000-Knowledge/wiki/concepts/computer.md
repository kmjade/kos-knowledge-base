# Computer

> UDC 004 — Computer science and technology.

## Definition

A computer is an electronic device that automatically executes arithmetic or logical operations according to programmed instructions. In knowledge organization, 004 is one of the largest subclasses in the UDC system, covering everything from hardware and software to artificial intelligence.
