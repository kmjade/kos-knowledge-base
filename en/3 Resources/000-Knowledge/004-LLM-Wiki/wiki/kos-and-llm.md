# Knowledge Organization and LLM

> Source: Compiled from Knowledge Organization and LLM raw.

## LLM's Impact on Knowledge Organization

Traditional knowledge organization relies on manually designed classification systems (UDC, DDC, etc.), while LLMs offer a semantic-driven approach:

### LLM Applications in Knowledge Management

### 1. Auto-tagging and Classification
- Input note content → LLM recommends UDC codes and tags
- Enables automatic note categorization
### 2. Semantic Search
- Natural language queries instead of exact keyword matching
- Cross-language retrieval

### Future Directions

- Personal knowledge graphs — Convert notes to RDF triples
- Agent-assisted research — LLM agents auto-collect, organize, and relate knowledge
- Dynamic classification — LLMs adapt classification structures as knowledge evolves
