# PARA + LLM-WIKI - Automating Personal Knowledge & Life Management

> **Resource:** The PARA + LLM-WIKI architecture for AI-driven personal knowledge management.

---

## Core Content

The PARA + LLM-WIKI architecture reimagines PKM through AI-driven automation.

**The Problem:**
Traditional note-taking fails due to information overload, unscalable manual organization, and tool fragmentation.

**The Solution - Three-Layer Architecture:**
1. **Perception Layer:** Omni-channel, frictionless capture
2. **Processing Layer:** LLM-powered routing, storage, extraction
3. **Application Layer:** RAG-based retrieval and synthesis

**Key Innovation:** Solves RAG pollution by building a noise-free professional knowledge sub-base.
