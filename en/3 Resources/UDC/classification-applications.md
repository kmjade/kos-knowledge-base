# Classification Applications

> **Resource:** Practical applications of classification in libraries, digital archives, and knowledge management systems.
> UDC: 025.4

---

## Core Content

Classification systems serve diverse practical applications across information management:

**Libraries:**
- Physical shelf arrangement (DDC, LCC, CLC)
- Subject cataloging and browsing
- Collection analysis and development

**Digital Archives:**
- Metadata schemas and crosswalks
- Faceted search and filtering
- Automated classification using AI

**Knowledge Management:**
- Content taxonomy for intranets and wikis
- Document routing and workflow
- Expertise location and communities of practice

The future of classification lies not in more precise trees, but in more intelligent networks.
