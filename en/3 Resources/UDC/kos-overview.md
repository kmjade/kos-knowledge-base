# Knowledge Organization Systems (KOS) Overview

> **Resource:** An overview of KOS including classification, thesauri, ontologies, and their relationships.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content

Knowledge Organization Systems (KOS) encompass all types of schemes for organizing information.

**KOS Spectrum (simple to complex):**
- **Subject Headings:** Flat list (LCSH)
- **Classification:** Hierarchical tree (DDC, UDC)
- **Taxonomy:** Controlled hierarchy
- **Thesaurus:** Network of concepts
- **Ontology:** Formal logic model (OWL)
- **Knowledge Graph:** Entity-relation graph (Wikidata)

KOS systems enable the DIKW progression (Data Information Knowledge Wisdom) by providing structure and context.
