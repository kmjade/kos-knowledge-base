# Knowledge Graph

> **Resource:** An overview of knowledge graphs, their structure, construction methods, and role in AI and search.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content
t

A knowledge graph represents knowledge as a network of entities and their relationships.

**Core Concepts:**
- **Nodes (Entities):** Real-world objects, concepts, events
- **Edges (Relationships):** Semantic links between entities
- **Triples (Statements):** Subject-Predicate-Object

**Types:**
- **Open/Public:** Wikidata, DBpedia, Google Knowledge Graph
- **Domain-specific:** Biomedical (UMLS), Financial
- **Enterprise:** Organizational knowledge base

**Construction:**
1. NLP extraction of entities and relations
2. Entity resolution across data sources
3. Fact validation and scoring
4. Continuous updates with new knowledge
