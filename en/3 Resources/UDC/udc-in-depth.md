## Core Content

The Universal Decimal Classification (UDC) is one of the most comprehensive knowledge classification systems, covering the entire universe of knowledge through a unique combination of **enumerative hierarchy** and **faceted synthesis**.

**Main Classes:**

| Class | Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 0 | General Knowledge | Meta-science, knowledge organization |
| 1 | Philosophy, Psychology | Logic, ethics, metaphysics |
| 2 | Religion, Theology | — |
| 3 | Social Sciences | Statistics, politics, economics |
| 4 | *(vacant)* | Originally linguistics, now merged into Class 8 |
| 5 | Mathematics, Natural Sciences | — |
| 6 | Applied Sciences | Technology, medicine, engineering |
| 7 | Arts, Recreation | — |
| 8 | Linguistics, Literature | — |
| 9 | Geography, History | — |

**Key Features:**

- **Auxiliary tables:** UDC's system of common and special auxiliaries enables precise, multi-dimensional classification
- **Faceted synthesis:** The colon operator (`:`) allows combination of multiple concepts (e.g., `001.8:004.8` for knowledge organization and AI)
- **International scope:** Unlike DDC's Anglo-American bias, UDC was designed for international use from its inception
