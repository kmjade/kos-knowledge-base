# LCC — Library of Congress Classification

> **Resource:** Overview of the Library of Congress Classification system, its structure, and differences from DDC.
> UDC: 025.4

---

## Core Content

The Library of Congress Classification (LCC) is widely used in academic and research libraries.

**Structure:**
- **Single letter:** Broad subject area (e.g., Q - Science)
- **Double letter:** Sub-discipline (e.g., QB - Astronomy, QC - Physics)
- **Cutter numbers:** Alphanumeric codes for specific topics

**Comparison with DDC:**
- LCC uses mixed alpha-numeric notation vs. DDC's pure numeric
- LCC was designed for a specific library's collection, not as a universal knowledge map
- LCC offers deeper granularity through Cutter numbers
- Updates are done per-volume independently
