# CLC — Chinese Library Classification

> **Resource:** An in-depth look at the Chinese Library Classification system, its unique features, and comparison with DDC.
> UDC: 025.4

---

## Core Content

The Chinese Library Classification (CLC) is the national standard classification system used in libraries across China.

**Main Classes:**
A - Marxism, Leninism, Maoism, Deng Xiaoping Theory
B - Philosophy, Religion
C - Social Sciences (General)
D - Politics, Law
E - Military
F - Economics
G - Culture, Science, Education
H - Language, Linguistics
I - Literature
J - Art
K - History, Geography
N-Z - Natural Sciences, Technology, Environment

**Comparison with DDC:**
- CLC uses mixed notation (Latin letters + Arabic digits)
- Class A establishes an independent Marxist/Leninist category
- Traditional Chinese Medicine has its own subclass (R2)
- Biography detail in K825.x is extremely fine-grained
- More granular for China-specific subjects than DDC
