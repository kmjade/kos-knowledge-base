# Personal Knowledge Management

> **Resource:** PKM strategies, tools, and methodologies for individuals.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content
t

Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is the practice of capturing, organizing, and retrieving information for personal learning.

**Key PKM Workflows:**
- **Capture:** Collect ideas and insights from reading
- **Organize:** Structure notes using PARA, Zettelkasten
- **Connect:** Create links between related ideas
- **Review:** Spaced repetition for reinforcement
- **Create:** Synthesize knowledge into new outputs

**Tools:** Obsidian, Roam, Notion, Anki

**KOS_LLM-Wiki Approach:** UDC for structure, PARA for workflow, wiki links for connections, LLM for automation.
**Method Comparison:**
| Method | Core Idea | Best For |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Zettelkasten | Atomic notes + bidirectional links | Academic writing |
| PARA | Organize by actionability | Project management |
| Progressive Summarization | Layer-by-layer distillation | Reading notes |
