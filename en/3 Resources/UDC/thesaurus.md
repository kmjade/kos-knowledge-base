# Thesaurus

> **Resource:** An introduction to thesauri as controlled vocabularies, their structure, relationships, and use in information retrieval.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content
t

A thesaurus is a controlled vocabulary that organizes terms by their semantic relationships.

**Relationship Types:**
- **BT (Broader Term):** Generic parent (e.g., Mammals BT Animals)
- **NT (Narrower Term):** Specific child (e.g., Dogs NT Mammals)
- **RT (Related Term):** Associative (e.g., Climate RT Weather)
- **UF (Use For):** Synonym management
- **USE:** Preferred term direction

**Standards:** ISO 25964 (thesauri), SKOS (Semantic Web representation)

Thesauri bridge the gap between classification hierarchies and formal ontologies.
**Standards:**
- ISO 25964: International standard for thesauri
- SKOS: W3C RDF standard for thesaurus representation

Thesauri bridge classification hierarchies and formal ontologies, adding rich semantic relationships while remaining human-readable.
