# Classification & Taxonomy

> **Resource:** An overview of classification and taxonomy as knowledge organization methods, their structures, and applications.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content

Classification is a fundamental method of knowledge organization that groups entities by shared properties into a structured hierarchy.

**Core Characteristics:**
- **Hierarchical structure:** From general (top) to specific (bottom)
- **Mutual exclusivity:** Each item belongs to exactly one class
- **Logical consistency:** Classification follows Aristotelian principles of division

**Relationship to Other KOS Types:**
- **Classification vs. Thesaurus:** Classification is hierarchical; thesauri add associative relationships
- **Classification vs. Ontology:** Classification is simpler; ontologies add formal logic and inference
- **Classification vs. Knowledge Graph:** Classification is the backbone; knowledge graphs connect entities across class boundaries
