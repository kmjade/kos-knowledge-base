# Knowledge Organization Systems

> **Resource:** A comprehensive overview of KOS types, relationships, and applications.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content

Knowledge Organization Systems (KOS) provide the structured frameworks for information discovery and retrieval.

**KOS Typology:**
1. Classification - Tree hierarchy (UDC, DDC)
2. Taxonomy - Controlled hierarchy (folder structure)
3. Thesaurus - Network (BT/NT/RT relationships)
4. Ontology - Formal logic (OWL-based models)
5. Knowledge Graph - Entity-relation (cross-note connections)

KOS_LLM-Wiki implements a multi-layered strategy: UDC as the universal map, PARA as the workflow layer, and wiki links as the personal connection layer.
