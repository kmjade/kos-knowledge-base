# DDC — Dewey Decimal Classification

> **Resource:** A comprehensive examination of the Dewey Decimal Classification system, its history, structure, and global impact.
> UDC: 025.4

---

## Core Content

The Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC) is the world's most widely used library classification system, created by Melvil Dewey in 1876.

**Main Classes:**
000 - Computer Science, Information & General Works
100 - Philosophy & Psychology
200 - Religion
300 - Social Sciences
400 - Language
500 - Science
600 - Technology
700 - Arts & Recreation
800 - Literature
900 - History & Geography

**Notable Features:**
- **Pure notation:** Only Arabic digits, no letters
- **Hierarchical expressiveness:** Each digit adds specificity
- **Auxiliary tables:** Standard subdivisions for form, place, language, etc.
- **Global adoption:** Used in over 200,000 libraries in 135+ countries
- **Criticism:** Anglo-American bias; interdisciplinary subjects forced into single positions
