# AI and Knowledge Systems

> **Resource:** The intersection of artificial intelligence and knowledge organization.
> UDC: 001.8

---

## Core Content
t

AI is transforming knowledge organization as both a subject and a tool:

**AI Applications in KOS:**
- **Automated Classification:** ML assigns subjects and categories
- **Entity Extraction:** NLP identifies entities in text
- **Clustering:** Unsupervised learning discovers categories
- **LLM-Powered Retrieval:** RAG finds and synthesizes knowledge

**The Symbiotic Relationship:**
- Better KOS -> Better AI training -> More intelligent systems
- Better AI -> More automated KOS -> More comprehensive knowledge bases
**AI Applications in KOS:**
- **Auto-classification:** ML classifiers for batch labeling
- **Entity Extraction:** NLP NER for people, places, organizations
- **Clustering:** Unsupervised learning for implicit categories
- **RAG:** Retrieve then generate — grounded in real documents
- **GraphRAG:** Knowledge graph retrieval — more precise relationships
